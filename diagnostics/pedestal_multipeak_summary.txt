PEDESTAL MULTI-PEAK STUDY -- th210 / th220 / th230
==================================================
Generated 2026-07-24 with diagnostics/plot_pedestal_multipeak.py on MERGED
histograms (low gain unless noted). A cell is one (slab, chip, channel, SCA)
pedestal histogram. Peak count = ROOT TSpectrum(10).Search(h, sigma=2.0,
"nobackground", threshold=0.30): a peak must be >=30% of the tallest peak's
height, and peaks closer than ~sigma bins merge. A cell counts as multi-peak
if >=2 peaks. Cells with <200 entries are skipped (peak count not trustworthy).

  Offset is ~equal in ADC in HG and LG (8 vs 7) even though HG gain is ~10x LG.
  An input-referred charge injection would give ~10x larger ADC offset in HG;
  it does not -> the step enters DOWNSTREAM of the gain split (SCA memory /
  common mode / ADC chain), which the preamp feedback capacitor cannot change.
  => A bigger gain capacitor does NOT fix the multi-peaks.

RELATED: LOW-GAIN MIP vs PEDESTAL (why the LG MIP fit is fragile)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  MIP MPV (pedestal-subtracted, i.e. the pedestal-to-MIP separation in ADC),
  median over well-fit channels of each threshold's MIP table:

               LG MPV (med)   LG IQR        HG MPV   HG/LG
    th230        4.34         [3.4,5.3]      31.58    7.3
    th220        3.79         [3.0,5.0]      21.69    5.7
    th210        4.76         [1.5,5.5]      18.55    3.9

  - In LG the MIP sits only ~4 ADC above pedestal at ALL thresholds -- a handful
    of ADC counts, comparable to the ~7 ADC multi-peak spacing and a few pedestal
    sigmas. The LG MIP fit is intrinsically fragile.
  - th230 -> th220: the LG separation shrinks (4.34 -> 3.79 ADC): pedestal
    approaches MIP as the threshold is lowered.
  - th210: the LG fit BIFURCATES. The MPV distribution is bimodal -- a large
    population pinned at ~1.5 ADC (ON the pedestal) and another at ~5.6 ADC. The
    median 4.76 sits in the valley and is misleading. The HG/LG ratio also drops
    to 3.9 (should be a hardware constant ~7), another sign the th210 LG MIP is
    unreliable.
  - A bigger LG feedback capacitor makes this WORSE (lower gain compresses the MIP
    further onto the pedestal). More LG gain would separate them but costs LG
    dynamic range. The robust route already in the repo: derive the LG gain from
    the HG<->LG anchor line (calibration/.../anchor/, plot_gain_anchor_fit.py),
    not from a direct LG MIP fit.
  Plot: diagnostics/compare/mip_lg_vs_pedestal_thresholds.png (gitignored).

CONCLUSIONS / OPEN QUESTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Universal result: a discrete multi-level baseline per channel, ~7-8 ADC
    spacing, same in both gains, independent of threshold. Ordering
    th220 (20.6%) > th210 (15.8%) > th230 (13.2%) is NOT monotonic in threshold
    -> not a trigger effect. th220 is the WORST, not the best, and MORE DATA
    does not shrink it (single-run th220 20.7% == 9-run merge 20.6%).
  - RETRACTED from the original th210 study (commit b94ecb8): "more frequent on
    high-index chips" and "SCA0 spike" -- both were single-run artifacts, gone
    after merging.
  - To AVOID masking ~20% of channels, the route is event-by-event common-mode
    subtraction -- but that only works if the baseline jumps are CORRELATED
    across a chip's channels within one acquisition. That needs an EVENT-LEVEL
    analysis of the decoded tuples (not these pedestal histograms) and is the
    next step.
